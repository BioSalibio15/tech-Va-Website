About Me

Behind Every Smooth-Running Team Is Reliable IT Support.

When someone can't log in, needs access, or has an issue that needs investigating, a clear and dependable point of contact makes the difference. That's the role I bring to remote teams.

Get to Know Me

My background is in IT infrastructure support. Day to day, that has meant troubleshooting technical problems, helping people regain access to their accounts, and handling IT service requests from start to finish.

I've worked with Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, and Microsoft 365, supporting things like password resets, MFA, account lockouts, shared mailbox and distribution list access, and access requests for enterprise tools. When an issue needs more than I can resolve, I escalate it clearly, with the details the next person needs.

I'm now focused on Technical Virtual Assistance. My goal is simple: help businesses and remote teams operate efficiently with reliable, well-communicated technical support.
